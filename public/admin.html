1. Connect YouTube

Stored encrypted on the server. Never sent to viewers. Enable YouTube Data API v3 in your Google Cloud project first.

2. Approve trusted sources

New short-video candidates from these channels can be published automatically when their audio-language tag matches. Review channels before approving; missing audio tags always need a video review.

Up to three enabled sources per language in this MVP.

3. Generate feeds

Discovery rotates through the content categories. Feeds include approved videos only, with no fallback across languages. Active feeds request a refresh every six hours. Nothing runs while the site is idle.

4. Review candidates

Watch and confirm the actual spoken language, short-video format, and suitability. Matching search terms or titles do not prove the language. Jobs videos are content candidates, not verified vacancies.

Interest signals

Anonymous sessions record impressions, playback time and 90% completions. Compare these signals by language and category. The mix aims for roughly 40% entertainment and 60% useful information when inventory allows.
