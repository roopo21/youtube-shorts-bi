Pool notes

The entertainment pool is a reproducibly random selection of 500 Hindi and 500 Marathi Shorts from public channel listings. The rebalanced pool spans 15 creators: Sony SAB is limited to 50 of the 500 Hindi records, and feed batches are capped at two videos per channel. It is an experimental playback pool, with source-assigned language labels. Each video’s duration is recorded when the embedded player first reports it. Not every embed has been individually tested.

Information reels are discovery candidates, not verified current advice or live listings. CNG queues and marriage/hair-growth advertising stay excluded as specified in your plan. Daily rates, local openings, alerts and local accommodation still require their field/API sources.

Views, actual playback seconds and completion are recorded by video, language and topic. More videos load as the viewer scrolls.
